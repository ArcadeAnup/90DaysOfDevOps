Day 90 of #90DaysOfDevOps 🚀

And the day is finally here.

Ninety days ago, I started this journey knowing almost nothing about the concepts and technologies that make up DevOps. Today, I can confidently say that I've built a strong foundation but more importantly, I've built the habit of showing up every single day.

The biggest lesson wasn't Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, or Cloud.

It was consistency.

Learning every day, even when you're not at your best. Showing up on the days you don't feel motivated. Taking one small step forward, even when it feels like you're making no progress.

You don't show up just to keep a streak alive or prove something to others. You show up for yourself.

And believe me it does get easier. The hardest part is simply putting one foot in front of the other, every single day.

Over these 90 days, I explored Linux, Git, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, Azure, AWS, networking, cloud computing, Infrastructure as Code, open source, technical writing, and content creation. I also built projects, created blogs, recorded videos, and learned by doing.

So... what's next?

I'll keep learning DevOps, dive deeper into the concepts that truly matter, contribute more to open source, and continue building projects that challenge me.

Because this isn't the end of the journey.

As The Beatles said:

"It's a long and winding road."


Thank you to everyone who followed along. 🚀
