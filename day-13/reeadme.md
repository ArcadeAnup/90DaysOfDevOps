Day 13 of #90DaysOfDevOps.

Today I got introduced to Docker and finally understood why containers became such a massive thing in modern development.

Learned about Docker Engine, Docker CLI, Docker Hub, Images and Containers, port mapping, and basic Docker commands. Also ran my first container using Nginx.

What stood out most was how lightweight containers are compared to Virtual Machines. Same idea of isolation, but way faster and more efficient.

Also explored:

* `docker run`
* `docker ps`
* `docker --version`
* `docker login`
* Port mapping with `-p 80:80`


Day 13 done.
