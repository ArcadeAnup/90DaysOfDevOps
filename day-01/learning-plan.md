## Who I Am

CS undergraduate. Absolute beginner in DevOps and Cloud, zero prior exposure. Starting from scratch, driven by genuine curiosity about how modern software is built, shipped, and scaled in the real world.

---

## What is DevOps & Cloud Engineering to Me?

DevOps is a set of practices that help reduce the time between the ideation phase of a product and its release in production to the end user, whether that's an internal team or a customer. Cloud Engineering is the infrastructure layer underneath all of it: scalable, on-demand compute that makes modern software possible.

I don't have a polished definition yet. That's fine. I'm here to build one through 90 days of real work.

## Why I'm Starting

Pure curiosity. I kept hearing about Kubernetes, CI/CD, AWS, and had no idea what any of it actually meant or how it fit together. That gap bothered me. So instead of watching from the outside, I'm stepping in. I'm jumping into an empty void in pursuit to find myself.

Long-term, this feeds directly into my goal of self-funding a Master's abroad. DevOps skills are hireable, real, and in demand.

## 3 Goals for 90 Days

1. **Contribute to an open-source DevOps project:** a real commit to a real repo, not a toy exercise.
2. **Build and ship a full CI/CD pipeline:** automated test, build, deploy, end to end.
3. **Deploy something on Kubernetes:** an actual running application, not just a tutorial screenshot.
